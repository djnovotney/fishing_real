# Fiji Pig Fishing v2.6

The reel now requires substantially faster clockwise rotation to hold pressure in green.

- Minimum target: approximately 86 RPM
- Ideal target: approximately 115 RPM
- Maximum target: approximately 143 RPM
- Below the range, pressure falls and the fish escapes.
- Inside the range, pressure stays green and the fish rises.
- Above the range, pressure rises and the line breaks.

A 1.2-second hook-set grace period gives the player time to grab the revealed handle and accelerate.
